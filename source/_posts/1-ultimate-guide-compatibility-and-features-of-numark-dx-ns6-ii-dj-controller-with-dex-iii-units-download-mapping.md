The **[Numark NS6II](https://www.numark.com/product/ns6ii)** is a premium cutting-edge 4-deck DJ controller with dual USB outs and built-in HD color displays at the center of each jog wheel. Now that our guru’s have created a custom map (download below) DJs can use the Numark NS6II for tactile, hands-on control over [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [DEX 3 RE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) DJ mixing software. DEX 3 (pro edition) is required for 4-deck mixing support.

With dual USB outputs, which provide DJs with the flexibility to connect two laptops simultaneously for seamless transitions between two DJs, and the ability to utilize the NS6II as a standalone DJ mixer, giving DJs the option to connect turntables, CD decks or other media devices, the NS6II is jam packed with unique professional features that place it in a class of it’s own at it’s price point (listed at $799 USD on most DJ equipment retailer websites).

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Numark NS6II front and rear](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/numark-ns6II-front-rear.jpg?fit=300%2C88&ssl=1 "Numark NS6II front and rear")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/numark-ns6II-front-rear.jpg?fit=1030%2C301&ssl=1)

The HD displays located at the center of each jog wheel (also found on the [Numark Mixtrack Platinum](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)) provide DJs with all the critical information required during a set to stay on point and stay engaged with the crowd.

The NS6II is equipped with a high-quality built-in audio interface that provides uncompromising 24-bit audio quality thanks to Numark’s audiophile-grade circuitry.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Numark NS6II angled](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/ns6ii.jpg?fit=300%2C188&ssl=1 "Numark NS6II angled")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/ns6ii.jpg?fit=1030%2C644&ssl=1)

The 4-deck DJ controllers has MPC performance pads that provide DEX 3 DJs with quick access to hot cues, auto loop/roll, manual loop mode, and sampler. The NS6II’s interface is rounded out with a silky-smooth 100 mm pitch faders and large, capacitive jog wheels that experienced [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [DEX 3 RE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) DJs will instantly appreciate.

Our DJ controller mapping team really enjoyed working with the NS6II and raved about the build quality. The controller should make a perfect pairing with DEX 3 for users that mix with all 4 decks, whether for audio or music video mixing (the crossfader will allow DJs to blend seamlessly from video to video).

### KEY NUMARK NS6II FEATURES:

* Dual USB ports
* 2-inch color LCD display integrated into each jog wheel.
* Digital mixer with two phono/line inputs that can be mixed directly without software
* Touch-capacitive knobs
* 6-inch multi-function jog wheel with smart learning capacitive technology
* 4-channel USB audio
* Stereo XLR outputs, master RCA outputs, booth RCA outputs
* Two 1/4 in. microphone inputs
* Stand-alone mixer 24-bit, 44.1K audio
* USB 2.0
* 4 in, 4 out with two phono preamps
* Replaceable crossfader
* Roll bar to protect front panel control knobs

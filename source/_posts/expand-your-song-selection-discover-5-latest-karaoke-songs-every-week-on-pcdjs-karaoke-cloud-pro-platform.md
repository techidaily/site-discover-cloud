[![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/kcp-5tunes-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/kcp-5tunes-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "kcp-5tunes-coverimage")

[Digitrax Entertainment](http://www.digitraxentertainment.com/) has set a new goal to release at least 5 tracks each-and-every-week into **Karaoke Cloud Pro**, the worlds best [professional karaoke subscription](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) platform accessible directly in our Karaoki [karaoke show hosting software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/).

This is fantastic news for both new and existing subscribers — _KJs can count on at least 20 karaoke songs added each month!_

The karaoke tracks released into the cloud each week will consist of brand spanking new karaoke releases or older core-catalog karaoke tunes that have recently been re-licensed by Digitrax to include in Karaoke Cloud Pro. File quality will never waver, as many of the best karaoke brands are included: DigiTrax Karaoke (formerly Chartbuster), SBI, Sunfly, Stage Stars, DK and Mr. Entertainer.

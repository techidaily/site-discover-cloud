The new 1.1.3 version of [LYRX](http://www.lyrxkaraoke.com) is largely a ‘maintenance release’. Since version 1.1.3 addresses a few bugs and improves stability and performance we suggest all users update immediately so your holiday karaoke hosting events are fun and problem free!

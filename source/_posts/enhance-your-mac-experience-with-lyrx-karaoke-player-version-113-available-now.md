**Version 1.1.3 of LYRX, the [top karaoke software for MAC](http://www.lyrxkaraoke.com), is now available for download!**

The new 1.1.3 version of [LYRX](http://www.lyrxkaraoke.com) is largely a ‘maintenance release’. We’ve popped the hood and fine tuned existing features to ensure better performance and overall stability. We’ve also fixed a crash some users reported with macOS High Sierra that would occur when opening LYRX and immediately loading/playing a song.

LYRX 1.1.3 is a free update for existing LYRX customers and will install directly over-top your existing installed version, leaving all your lists and settings intact. Since version 1.1.3 addresses a few bugs and improves stability and performance we suggest all users update immediately so your holiday karaoke hosting events are fun and problem free!
